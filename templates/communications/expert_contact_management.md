## Expert Contact Information

Use this template to track cultural experts for the Kikuyu proverb validation project.

| Expert Name | Role/Title | Institution/Community | Email | Phone | Status | Date Contacted | Follow-up Date | Notes |

### Status Categories:
- **Identified** - Potential expert identified
- **Contacted** - Initial email sent
- **Interested** - Expressed interest in participating
- **Confirmed** - Agreed to participate, materials sent
- **In Progress** - Currently reviewing materials
- **Completed** - Finished review process
- **Declined** - Politely declined participation
- **No Response** - No response after follow-ups

## Recruitment Strategy

### Phase 1: Primary Targets (3-5 experts)
Focus on highly qualified experts with strong cultural and academic backgrounds.

**Target Profile:**
- Native Kikuyu speakers
- Advanced education or recognized cultural authority
- Experience with translation or cultural interpretation
- Available within project timeline

### Phase 2: Secondary Targets (5-10 experts)
Expand to broader pool of qualified cultural experts.

**Target Profile:**
- Kikuyu cultural knowledge
- Professional or community recognition
- Interest in cultural preservation
- Flexibility with timeline and format

### Phase 3: Community Outreach
If needed, reach out through community networks and recommendations.

## Communication Tracking

### Email Template Usage:
1. **Initial Contact:** `expert_recruitment_email.md`
2. **Follow-up:** `expert_followup_templates.md` (1-week follow-up)
3. **Onboarding:** `expert_onboarding_email.md`
4. **Support:** Various templates as needed
5. **Completion:** Thank you and next steps

### Response Management:
- **Positive Response:** Schedule call, send materials
- **Questions:** Use question response template
- **Hesitation:** Offer flexibility, reduced scope
- **Decline:** Thank them, ask for recommendations
- **No Response:** Follow up after 1 week, final follow-up after 3 weeks

## Quality Assurance

### Minimum Expert Qualifications:
- [ ] Native or near-native Kikuyu proficiency
- [ ] Cultural knowledge and context understanding
- [ ] Professional or community recognition
- [ ] Available for 8-12 hour commitment
- [ ] Comfortable with Excel or alternative formats

### Validation Approach:
- **Multiple Expert Review:** Critical proverbs reviewed by 2+ experts
- **Consistency Checks:** Compare expert interpretations
- **Cultural Authority:** Prioritize recognized cultural leaders
- **Academic Rigor:** Include academic experts for methodology

## Contact Script for Phone Calls

### Introduction:
"Hello [Name], my name is Charles Ndethi Kibaki. I'm conducting research on traditional Kikuyu proverbs for my master's thesis, and I was hoping to speak with you about potentially participating as a cultural expert."

### Key Points to Cover:
- Research purpose and academic context
- Cultural preservation goals
- Expert role and time commitment
- Compensation and recognition
- Flexibility with timeline and format
- Respect for traditional knowledge

### Questions to Ask:
- Interest level in cultural preservation work
- Experience with proverb interpretation
- Availability and preferred timeline
- Questions about the research
- Preferred communication method
