# Expert Communication Templates

This directory contains email templates for managing cultural expert recruitment and collaboration for the Kikuyu proverb research project.

## Template Files

### 1. **`expert_recruitment_email.md`**
Initial outreach email for recruiting cultural experts.

**Use Case:** First contact with potential expert reviewers  
**Key Features:**
- Professional project overview
- Clear role expectations
- Flexible compensation discussion
- Cultural sensitivity emphasis

### 2. **`expert_onboarding_email.md`**
Welcome email with materials and instructions for confirmed experts.

**Use Case:** Onboarding confirmed participants  
**Key Features:**
- Material distribution
- Clear timeline expectations
- Support contact information
- Cultural integrity commitment

### 3. **`expert_followup_templates.md`**
Various follow-up emails for different scenarios.

**Use Case:** Ongoing communication during review process  
**Includes:**
- Progress check-ins
- Question responses
- Completion reminders
- Thank you messages

## Customization Guidelines

### Personal Information to Update:
- `[Expert Name]` - Replace with actual expert's name
- `[Your Email]` - Your contact email address
- `[Your Phone]` - Your phone number for urgent contact
- `[Compensation Details]` - Specific payment information
- `[Institution Details]` - Full institutional affiliation

### Cultural Considerations:
- Use appropriate Kikuyu greetings when culturally relevant
- Respect traditional forms of address for elders
- Acknowledge community standing and expertise
- Emphasize cultural preservation goals

### Professional Adaptations:
- **Academic Experts:** Emphasize research collaboration and publication potential
- **Community Elders:** Focus on cultural preservation and community benefit
- **Business Professionals:** Highlight practical applications and modern relevance

## Usage Workflow

1. **Initial Contact:** Use `expert_recruitment_email.md`
2. **Follow-up:** Adapt follow-up templates as needed
3. **Confirmed Participation:** Send `expert_onboarding_email.md` with materials
4. **Ongoing Support:** Use follow-up templates for communication
5. **Completion:** Send thank you and next steps

## Quality Assurance

### Before Sending:
- [ ] Personalize all bracketed placeholders
- [ ] Verify attached materials are current
- [ ] Check cultural sensitivity of language
- [ ] Confirm contact information is accurate
- [ ] Review timeline and compensation details

### Cultural Sensitivity Checklist:
- [ ] Respectful tone and language
- [ ] Appropriate acknowledgment of expertise
- [ ] Clear cultural preservation goals
- [ ] Commitment to traditional knowledge respect
- [ ] Professional but warm communication style

## Integration with Expert Review System

These templates support the expert review workflow:

```
Recruitment → Onboarding → Review Process → Follow-up → Completion
     ↓              ↓             ↓             ↓           ↓
Template 1    Template 2    Support Emails  Check-ins   Thank You
```
