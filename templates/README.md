# Project Templates

This directory contains reusable templates for various aspects of the thiLLMo research project.

## Directory Structure

### `/communications/`
Email templates and communication management for expert recruitment and collaboration.

**Files:**
- `expert_recruitment_email.md` - Initial outreach to cultural experts
- `expert_onboarding_email.md` - Welcome and materials distribution
- `expert_followup_templates.md` - Various follow-up communication templates
- `expert_contact_management.md` - Contact tracking and recruitment strategy
- `README.md` - Communication template documentation

## Template Categories

### 1. Expert Communication Templates
Professional email templates for recruiting and managing cultural expert participation in the proverb validation process.

**Key Features:**
- Culturally sensitive language
- Professional academic tone
- Flexible compensation discussions
- Clear role expectations
- Support and follow-up frameworks

**Usage Context:**
- Recruiting qualified Kikuyu cultural experts
- Onboarding confirmed participants
- Ongoing communication during review process
- Managing technical and cultural questions

## Using the Templates

### Before Using Any Template:
1. **Review cultural sensitivity** guidelines
2. **Customize all bracketed placeholders** with specific information
3. **Verify contact information** and institutional details
4. **Check compensation and timeline** details
5. **Ensure appropriate tone** for recipient relationship

### Template Customization:
- Replace `[Expert Name]` with actual expert's name
- Update `[Your Email]` and contact information
- Specify `[Compensation Details]` appropriately
- Adjust tone for academic vs. community experts
- Include relevant cultural greetings when appropriate

## Quality Standards

### Cultural Sensitivity Requirements:
- Respectful acknowledgment of traditional knowledge
- Appropriate recognition of expert authority
- Clear cultural preservation goals
- Professional but warm communication style
- Commitment to ethical research practices

### Communication Best Practices:
- Clear, professional language
- Specific timeline expectations
- Flexible accommodation offers
- Multiple contact options
- Prompt response commitments
