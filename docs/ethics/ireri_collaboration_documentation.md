## 6. VALIDATION LIMITATIONS

### 6.1 Single-Expert Validation

**Current Limitation:**
This project relies on a single cultural expert (Ireri Mbaabu) for primary validation, rather than the ideal multi-expert panel (3-5 experts) recommended in ontology development guidelines.

**Implications:**
- Lower statistical confidence in inter-rater agreement
- Limited geographic/dialectal representation
- Single perspective on contested interpretations
- Potential individual bias in cultural weight estimates
- Reduced generalizability claims

**Mitigation Strategies:**
1. **Deep Documentation:**
   - Extensive context documentation per proverb
   - Detailed rationale for interpretations
   - Regional variation notes where known
   - Uncertainty acknowledgment where applicable

2. **Transparency:**
   - Clear documentation of single-expert approach in thesis
   - Honest reporting of validation limitations
   - Discussion of implications for findings
   - Recommendations for future multi-expert validation

3. **Prioritized Secondary Validation:**
   - Seek 2nd validator for Tier 1 concepts (wealth, poverty - 30 failures)
   - Focus limited resources on highest-impact concepts
   - Document validation status clearly (single vs. multiple experts)

4. **Quality Over Quantity:**
   - Prefer fewer well-documented proverbs
   - Deep cultural context over broad coverage
   - High confidence in included items
   - Explicit uncertainty markers for ambiguous cases
