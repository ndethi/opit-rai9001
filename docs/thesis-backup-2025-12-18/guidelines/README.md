# OPIT Thesis Guidelines

This directory contains the official thesis guidelines and templates from the Open Institute of Technology (OPIT) for the MSc Responsible AI program.

## Files

### `OPIT_Dissertation_Template.docx`
- **Purpose**: Official dissertation template from OPIT
- **Format**: Microsoft Word document
- **Usage**: Template for thesis structure, formatting, and layout requirements
- **Downloaded**: September 19, 2025

### `OPIT_Thesis_Sessions_2025-26.pdf`
- **Purpose**: Thesis session guidelines for academic year 2025-26
- **Format**: PDF document
- **Usage**: Guidelines for thesis supervision sessions, milestones, and deadlines
- **Downloaded**: September 19, 2025

### `OPIT_Thesis_Sessions_2025-26_original.pdf`
- **Purpose**: Original version of thesis sessions guidelines
- **Format**: PDF document
- **Usage**: Backup copy of the thesis sessions guidelines
- **Downloaded**: September 19, 2025

## Integration with thiLLMo Project

These guidelines inform the structure and requirements for the thiLLMo thesis:
- **Academic Standards**: Ensure compliance with OPIT formatting requirements
- **Timeline**: Align development milestones with thesis submission deadlines
- **Structure**: Use template structure for consistent academic presentation
- **Supervision**: Follow session guidelines for advisor meetings and progress tracking

1. **Template Adherence**: Follow the dissertation template for consistent formatting
