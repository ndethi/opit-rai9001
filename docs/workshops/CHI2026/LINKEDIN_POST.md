---

## The Problem We Don't Talk About Enough

Imagine this: You ask ChatGPT to translate a Kikuyu proverb:

**"Andu ni indo"** 

The literal translation? "People are wealth."

Technically correct. But here's what's missing: In Kikuyu culture, this isn't about money or possessions. It's a profound teaching that **community relationships and social capital are the true measure of prosperity**—a worldview fundamentally different from Western individualism.

Standard AI translation? It gets the words right but **completely misses the soul**.

---

## Why Traditional Metrics Fail Us

We discovered something fascinating (and troubling): The industry-standard translation metrics—BLEU and SACREBLEU—are essentially **counting word matches**. They tell us nothing about cultural authenticity.

You could have a perfect BLEU score and still lose everything that makes a proverb meaningful to the people who've carried it for generations.

**That's not translation. That's cultural erasure dressed up as technology.**

---

## Our Solution: Teaching AI to Learn Culture

We built **thiLLMo** (combining "thimo" - Kikuyu for proverbs - with "LLM"):

Instead of just feeding GPT-4 text, we created a **knowledge graph** - think of it as a cultural memory map - encoding 100 Kikuyu proverbs with their:
- Cultural themes (like *utonga* - holistic wealth: material + social)
- Metaphorical meanings
- Usage contexts
- Relationships to broader cultural concepts

The results?
✅ **10.5% improvement** in cultural authenticity over standard GPT-4 (statistically significant: p < 0.000001)
✅ **7.4% improvement** over traditional retrieval methods

More importantly: When translating "Andu ni indo," our system adds "**People are the TRUE wealth**"—that one word "true" preserves the cultural teaching that community, not currency, defines prosperity.

---

## The Honest Part: What We Still Don't Know

Here's where I'll be real with you:

**Our biggest limitation?** All our evaluation was automated. 

We built sophisticated metrics, used AI judges, compared systems... but we didn't do formal community validation with Kikuyu elders and cultural experts. 

**That's the work that matters most.**

Because here's the truth: **You can't validate cultural authenticity without the culture**.

## Why This Matters Beyond Tech

We're living through an AI revolution. But if we're not careful, it'll be a revolution that **erases more cultures than it empowers**.

8 million people speak Kikuyu. Billions speak languages that AI barely acknowledges. Their wisdom, their proverbs, their worldviews—they deserve better than word-by-word translation.

They deserve systems built **with** them, not just **for** them.
